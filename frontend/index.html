Survival Guides

DISASTER PROTOCOLS

Flood Protocol
  • Move to the highest floor or roof immediately.
  • Disconnect the main electrical switch.
  • Do not walk through moving water (6 inches can sweep you away).
Cyclone Protocol
  • Stay indoors, away from all windows and glass.
  • Move to an interior hallway or bathroom.
  • Keep your phone on maximum battery saver mode.
Earthquake Protocol
  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On under a sturdy desk or table.
  • Stay away from glass, windows, outside doors and walls.
  • If outdoors, move to an open area away from trees, buildings, and power lines.

FIRST AID

Severe Bleeding
  • Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth.
  • Keep pressing continuously for at least 5 minutes.
  • Elevate the injured area above the heart if possible.
Broken Bones
  • Do not attempt to realign the bone.
  • Immobilize the area using rolled-up magazines or thick cloth as a splint.
  • Apply a cold pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling.
Severe Burns
  • Cool the burn under cool (not cold) running water for 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Remove rings or tight items from the burned area immediately.
  • Cover the area loosely with sterile, non-fluffy gauze.

Survival Tips

01
Purify All Water Boil any tap or flood water for at least 1 full minute before drinking to avoid waterborne diseases like cholera.
02
Conserve Battery Turn your phone to ultra battery saver mode, dim the screen to the lowest legible setting, and disable background data.
03
Electrical Hazards Never touch wet electrical switches or panels. If water enters your home, turn off the main breaker immediately.
04
Beware of Wildlife Rising floodwaters displace snakes and insects. Tap the ground with a stick before stepping and check inside shoes.
05
Food Safety Discard any food, even canned goods, that has come into direct contact with floodwater.
06
Stay Dry & Warm Hypothermia can happen even in warm Indian climates if you remain wet for hours. Prioritize keeping your core dry.
07
Visual Signaling If trapped on a roof, hang brightly colored clothes or use a mirror/glass to reflect sunlight at passing rescue helicopters.
08
Structural Safety During a cyclone, stay away from all glass windows. Keep interior doors closed and brace exterior doors if possible.
